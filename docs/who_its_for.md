# Who SciNetX is for

SciNetX is built for people who need a structured way to review bibliometric and network analysis results.

## Research teams
SciNetX helps research groups organize results into summaries, figures, tables, and network views that can be reviewed, discussed, and exported.

## Core facilities and shared services
Teams that support multiple investigators can use SciNetX as a consistent way to deliver and review analysis outputs across projects.

## Institutions and programs
SciNetX can support repeatable reporting, portfolio review, landscape analysis, and internal evaluation work where clear output packages matter.

## Commercial users
Commercial use is available under a separate agreement. If you are evaluating SciNetX for internal use, customer-facing delivery, or a hosted service, start with the access page and licensing summary.

## Before access
You can review the product overview, workflow, citation information, and launch materials on [scinetx.com](https://scinetx.com) before requesting access.

## After access
Onboarding and licensed delivery are available through [scinetx.com](https://scinetx.com).
